About

Hey Virgil is currently a creative collaboration between Sarah Dzida & Jon Saltzman about the new ways humans can explore, make and engage with products and emerging tech in agentic, human ways. It’s an evolving experiment in real-time.

Sarah Dzida is a strategy consultant with 20+ years of turning complex, messy ideas into real products, experiences and systems that transform how businesses use technology to grow. Her clients include global brands like Walmart, Gensler, Lexus, and the City of Los Angeles to startups like DumDumAI, Storyshift, and Motive Labs. In addition to her professional work, she’s a teacher, creative writer, and artist.
